## Research Philosophy

I place strong emphasis on **data quality, biological interpretation, visual understanding, and rigorous validation**.

A computational result is only useful when the underlying data, experimental design, statistical model, and biological interpretation are reliable. If poor-quality data enter the pipeline, the result is the well-known GIGO effect: **Garbage In, Garbage Out**. My work therefore includes extensive quality control, batch-effect evaluation, validation of analytical assumptions, and development of reproducible workflows designed to minimize avoidable sources of error.

## Current Technical Interests

I am particularly interested in integrating:

**DNA alterations → RNA expression and splicing → transcript isoforms → protein-domain analysis → pathways → single-cell and spatial localization → prediction of functional consequences → therapeutic development**

The goal is to trace the flow of biological information from patient-specific genomic alterations to functional tumor phenotypes, and ultimately to help predict and prioritize the most appropriate therapeutic strategies.
